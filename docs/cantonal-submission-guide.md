# Swiss Cantonal Tax Filing & Submission Guide (Cantons ZH, BE, ZG, SZ, SG, AG, BS)

This document specifies the official submission routes, electronic portals, and postal procedures for tax returns in the seven primary cantons supported by SunTax.

---

## Overview of Swiss Tax Filing Workflow

In the Swiss Confederation, individual income and wealth taxes are assessed jointly by three levels of government:
1. **Federal Tax (Direkte Bundessteuer)**: Governed by the Federal Direct Tax Act (DBG/LIFD).
2. **Cantonal Tax (Staatssteuer / Kantonssteuer)**: Governed by each canton's tax statute (StG).
3. **Municipal Tax (Gemeindesteuer)**: Calculated via the local municipal multiplier (*Steuerfuss*).

Tax returns are filed with the taxpayer's canton of residence as of December 31st of the tax year. The cantonal tax authority assesses both cantonal/communal taxes and federal direct tax in a unified assessment (*Veranlagungsverfügung*).

## Cantonal Portals & Filing Procedures

### 1. Canton Zürich (ZH)
- **Official Online Portal**: [zhservices.ch / eTax.zh](https://www.zh.ch/de/steuern-finanzen/steuern/steuern-natuerliche-personen/elektronische-steuererklaerung-zhservices.html)
- **Competent Tax Authority**: *Kantonales Steueramt Zürich / Gemeindesteueramt Ihrer Wohngemeinde*
- **Electronic Filing Process**:
  1. Access *eTax.zh* via ZHservices login (using canton tax accession code / PIN from the official filing invitation).
  2. Enter or verify declared figures matching SunTax's itemized breakdown (Lohnausweis, account balances, securities, Pillar 3a).
  3. Upload supporting PDF documents (SunTax Summary PDF, bank statements, certificates).
  4. Submit electronically without physical signature (paperless via electronic verification).
- **Postal Filing Alternative**:
  - Print the completed tax declaration and sign the *Freigabequittung* (submission declaration).
  - Enclose original salary certificate (*Lohnausweis*) and bank interest/balance statements.
  - Mail to the municipal tax office (*Gemeindesteueramt*) of your municipality.

---

### 2. Canton Bern (BE)
- **Official Online Portal**: [TaxMe-Online via BE-Login](https://www.taxme.ch)
- **Competent Tax Authority**: *Steuerverwaltung des Kantons Bern, Postfach 8334, 3001 Bern*
- **Electronic Filing Process**:
  1. Log in to *TaxMe-Online* via BE-Login using the personal ID and access code from the tax declaration form.
  2. Input or review figures corresponding to the SunTax calculation results.
  3. Upload supporting PDF evidence directly into TaxMe.
  4. Finalize submission electronically. When e-signature is activated, no paper is required; otherwise print and sign the one-page *Freigabequittung*.
- **Postal Filing Alternative**:
  - If filing on paper, send the signed *Freigabequittung* with SunTax summary and required attachments to the Tax Administration of Canton Bern.

---

### 3. Canton Zug (ZG)
- **Official Online Portal**: [eTax.zug](https://www.zg.ch/behoerden/finanzdirektion/steuerverwaltung/etax-zug)
- **Competent Tax Authority**: *Kantonale Steuerverwaltung Zug, Aabachstrasse 5, 6300 Zug*
- **Electronic Filing Process**:
  1. Open *eTax.zug* using PID (Personen-Identifikationsnummer) and password provided on the tax declaration header.
  2. Transfer verified items from SunTax declaration.
  3. Attach PDF documents directly in the eTax upload section.
  4. Transmit online.
- **Postal Filing Alternative**:
  - Print the summary sheet, sign by hand (both spouses if married), and send with original proofs to the Zug Cantonal Tax Administration.

---

### 4. Canton Basel-Stadt (BS)
- **Official Online Portal**: [BalTax / eTax Basel-Stadt](https://www.steuerverwaltung.bs.ch/steuererklaerung/elektronische-steuererklaerung.html)
- **Competent Tax Authority**: *Steuerverwaltung Basel-Stadt, Fischmarkt 10, Postfach, 4001 Basel*
- **Electronic Filing Process**:
  1. Access *BalTax Online* with the PIN from your tax declaration letter.
  2. Enter declared income, wealth, and itemized deductions as prepared in SunTax.
  3. Upload digital attachments.
  4. Transmit electronically.
- **Postal Filing Alternative**:
  - Print the *Deklaration* summary page, sign, and mail to *Steuerverwaltung Basel-Stadt*.

---

### 5. Canton Aargau (AG)
- **Official Online Portal**: [SmartTax / EasyTax Aargau](https://www.ag.ch/de/verwaltung/dfr/steuern/smarttax)
- **Competent Tax Authority**: *Kantonales Steueramt Aargau / Gemeindesteueramt*
- **Electronic Filing Process**:
  1. Access *SmartTax* online using the access code from the official tax packet (*Steuererklärung*).
  2. Enter items matching the SunTax calculated lines.
  3. Attach electronic evidence.
  4. Transmit via the portal.
- **Postal Filing Alternative**:
  - Print and sign the submission confirmation sheet (*Einreichequittung*) and submit to the local municipal tax office.

---

### 6. Canton St. Gallen (SG)
- **Official Online Portal**: [eTax.sg](https://www.sg.ch/steuern-finanzen/steuern/steuern-fuer-privatpersonen/elektronische-steuererklaerung.html)
- **Competent Tax Authority**: *Kantonales Steueramt St. Gallen / Zuständiges Gemeindesteueramt*
- **Electronic Filing Process**:
  1. Access *eTax.sg* using the security code (*Sicherheitscode*) printed on the tax form.
  2. Match figures against SunTax's verified tax profile.
  3. Submit declaration online and attach required PDF certificates.
- **Postal Filing Alternative**:
  - Mail the signed submission receipt with supporting documentation to the communal tax administration.

---

### 7. Canton Schwyz (SZ)
- **Official Online Portal**: [eTax.sz](https://www.sz.ch/behoerden/finanzdepartement/kantonales-steueramt/steuern-natuerliche-personen/etax-sz.html)
- **Competent Tax Authority**: *Kantonales Steueramt Schwyz, Bahnhofstrasse 15, Postfach 1232, 6431 Schwyz*
- **Electronic Filing Process**:
  1. Access *eTax.sz* using the register code (*Register-Nummer*) and PIN.
  2. Input data prepared and checked in SunTax.
  3. Submit electronically or print the signed transmission confirmation.
- **Postal Filing Alternative**:
  - Submit the signed declaration form with salary certificates and asset proofs to the district tax office (*Bezirkssteueramt*).

## Export File Standards & Interoperability

- **Official PDF Filing Summary**: SunTax produces an A4 itemized filing summary detailing taxpayer identity, gross incomes, statutory deductions, net taxable values, and line-by-line tax computations citing cantonal and federal tax articles.
- **eCH Structured Data Export**: An eCH-compliant structured XML export (`suntaxTaxDataExport`) adhering to eCH-0196 / eCH-0119 standards for electronic tax declaration data exchange and record portability.
