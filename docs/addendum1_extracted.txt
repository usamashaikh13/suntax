SunTax – Developer Guide · Addendum 1
9 November 2026 · Only the additions to the guide you already have. Files: Ergaenzung_1.zip
1. Brand name in one place
The product will be renamed («SunTax» is only a working name). Never hard-code the name:
keep it, the logo, the domain and the contact e-mail in one config value each, used everywhere
(UI, e-mails, PDFs, sourceDescription  in eCH-0119).
2. Cantons switched on one by one
Build the app for all cantons (rules for all 26 are in the data), but submission is only offered
in cantons that are switched on.
Each canton gets an availability flag per tax year in the rules table: available  (direct
submission works) or coming_soon . Switching a canton on must need no code change,
only data.
First confirmed: Zürich (eCH-0119 via webservice from 1 Jan 2027, access requested at zh-
tax@zh.ch). Other cantons are switched on as soon as their access or certification is in
place (candidates: Bern, Thurgau, Schwyz, Solothurn). The goal is to launch with as many
cantons as are ready by then.
The transmission to each canton is its own small module (same eCH-0119 file, different
delivery), so adding a canton does not touch the calculation.
If the postcode on 31 Dec is in a coming_soon  canton: show «Dein Kanton kommt bald»,
offer a waiting list by e-mail (canton + e-mail stored), no payment.
The website's canton calculator keeps showing all 26 cantons (it only informs).
3. Rules 2025 and 2026 as data rules_2025_2026.json
All 26 cantons with base values = 2025 and a y2026  block with only the changes. Confirmed
for 2026: federal tax, ZH, AG, SZ, SO, VS (changed) and UR, GL, BS, VD (unchanged). The
other 17 cantons: 2026 not yet confirmed, use 2025 until updated. Federal car rate 2026: CHF
0.75/km (2025: 0.70).
4. Receipt rules as data receipts_rules.json
For every field: required  (attached, submission blocked without it) or keep_only  (not
attached). Plus the receipts that are always required, the annex documents the app generates
itself, and the required personal data. Values follow Zürich; store them per canton and year.
5. Question catalogue questions.json
The 14 question cards of step 3 with exact German wording, answer options, follow-up fields
and the condition for each card (e.g. custody questions only for unmarried parents, partner-2

questions only for married couples). The «Next» button stays disabled until the card is

6. All app texts in one file app_texts_de.json
250 visible German texts extracted from the prototype, so nothing has to be retyped. Put all UI

7. New feature: «So viel sparst du» (savings display)
Goal: the user sees what each deduction is worth in francs. This is the main argument against
the free cantonal software.
Per deduction: next to every optional deduction (commute, meals, pillar 3a, insurance,
training, child care, donations, medical costs …) show «Spart ca. CHF X».
How to compute X: run the tax engine twice, once with and once without that one
deduction, for both cantonal and federal tax: X = (tax without) − (tax with). Round to CHF
10. Never use a flat percentage.
Summary at the end (review step and after submission): «Dank deinen Abzügen sparst du
ca. CHF Y.» Y = tax without all optional deductions − tax with them (computed once, not
the sum of the single X values, because of progression).
Wording: always «ca.» and the note «Unverbindliche Schätzung – die definitive Steuer
berechnet das Steueramt.»
Dependency: the cantonal part is only as good as the cantonal tariff. Until real cantonal
tariffs and municipal multipliers are implemented, label the amount clearly as a rough
estimate.
Tests: X is never negative, is 0 when the deduction is 0, and Y ≥ the largest single X.
